Now

On encryption strength alone, Signal's design is stronger than Tegami's. Where Tegami differs: no phone number needed, no ads or operator AI, your own AI can appear as a friend, and the server-side code is public too (of the four apps, only Signal also publishes its server code).

Not yet

Tegami is browser-only for now, with no app-store apps. It also has no forward secrecy.

Among the apps compared, we could not open the official help pages to fully verify WhatsApp's encryption details and source code status, or Signal's AI features.

Next

  • ConsideringTegami is run by its developer as an individual today. We are also working out how conversations can survive if the developer can no longer continue, and how the project could move to a separate organization later.
How is Tegami different from 0xchat?
Now

0xchat is an app with nearly every Nostr chat feature, and it heads in a different direction from Tegami. The comparison below is based on what we checked in 0xchat's public repository (0xchat-app/0xchat-app-main) code on 2026-11-16.

0xchatTegami
Who you can reachAnyone on Nostr. Uses public relays (damus, nos.lol, etc.); its own relay was shut downOnly people registered with Tegami. Dedicated relay
Secret key storageEncrypted with an app-generated password, which is stored in the same database row and opened automatically at launch. A separate app lock (biometrics, PIN) existsEncrypted with passkey PRF; passkey authentication required on every open (where PRF is unsupported, encrypted with an on-device key)
External signersSupports NIP-46 and NIP-55 (Amber, etc.)Not supported
Key exportYou can show and copy your nsec in SettingsNot possible
If you lose your keyNo backup mechanism found. Assumes you store your nsec yourselfRestore with a recovery code
AITranslation only (on-device or LibreTranslate). No AI bots or MCP found in the codeYour own PC's AI appears as a friend. MCP support. The app controls what the AI is allowed to do
GroupsNIP-17 groups, NIP-28 channels, NIP-29 relay-based groups. MLS supported in the sister app XChatNIP-17 groups only
CallsWebRTC via NIP-100. Users configure their own TURN serverWebRTC with signaling in encrypted envelopes. No TURN server yet
PlatformsApps for iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux. No browser version foundBrowser only for now. Apps in progress
OtherBuilt-in Tor, Cashu wallet, zaps, translationRegistration via invite codes and human checks; key suspension by the operator

0xchat is an app for people who already use Nostr, while Tegami is for people who want to leave LINE without knowing anything about Nostr. 0xchat is further ahead in openness to the Nostr world and in fine-grained settings.

Not yet

Tegami has no external signers, no key export, no app-store apps, and no TURN server.

We could not fully verify whether 0xchat's entire database is encrypted (there is a screen for entering a database passphrase).

Next
  • Built, not releasedTURN server. Android app prototype (runs on an emulator as far as the chat list).
  • DecidedWe will ship apps for iPhone, Android, Windows, and Mac.
  • ConsideringExternal signers (NIP-46), and how to connect with other Nostr apps.
How is Tegami different from White Noise?
Now

White Noise is a messaging app that uses MLS on Nostr. It can also let you talk to an AI running on your own machine as a contact, and supports multiple profiles. As encryption goes, it has forward secrecy, so it is stronger than Tegami's NIP-17. Where Tegami differs: it protects keys with passkeys, has calls, runs a dedicated relay with registration and reports, and lets you decide in detail, inside the app, what an AI is allowed to do. (White Noise's official docs as of 2026-11-13 did not mention passkeys, calls, or MCP.)

Not yet

On encryption strength, Tegami falls short of White Noise.

So what does only Tegami have?
Now

Taken one at a time, every feature exists in some other app. What Tegami aims for is the combination: someone who wants to leave LINE but has never heard of Nostr can start with just a passkey and no password, see family and their own AI in the same chat list, and use it in a place protected from spam.

Next
  • DecidedWe want Tegami to be the app people simply use for messaging by default, not one they pick for a single feature.
