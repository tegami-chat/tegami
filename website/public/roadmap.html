Roadmap

What we've built, and what's next.

We release what can be taken back first. Anything that can't be taken back comes last.

Suggest a feature on GitHub (opens in a new tab)
✓Released

Everyday messaging

One-on-one and group chats, photos, videos, files, voice messages, stickers, replies and search.

✓Released

Voice and video calls

Call anyone in your chat list. You get a notification when someone calls.

✓Released

Encryption and passkeys

Messages are encrypted on your device. Your account is protected by a passkey, with no password.

✓Released

Recovery code and new phones

Bring your account back with a recovery code, or move it to another device in a few taps.

✓Released

Multiple accounts

Use several accounts on one device and switch between them.

✓Released

Your AI as a friend

A small program (tegami-agent) lets the AI on your computer join your chat list. Works with MCP.

✓Released

Block, report and a box for strangers

Messages from people you don't know wait in a separate box. Block and report anyone.

✓Released

Open sign-up with invites

Anyone can join. Messages from people who joined without an invite do not show up in the strangers box. Add them as a friend and you can chat.

✓Released

Connect your AI with a QR code

Run one command on your computer and scan the QR code it shows with the app. Your AI is added as a friend.

◆Built, not released

AI in group chats

Invite an AI into a group once everyone agrees, and let AIs talk to each other when you allow it. The code is written, but this version does not allow it. It will come in a later version.

●In progress

Your own address

An easy-to-share address like name@tegami.chat.

●In progress

iPhone and Android apps

Native apps with a real ringing screen for calls.

○Planned

Disappearing messages

Choose to send a message that disappears after a while.

○Planned

Windows, Mac and Linux apps

Desktop apps you can keep open all day.

○Planned

Tegami API

Read and send messages from your own programs.

○Planned

Build your own screens

Make the screens you like on top of Tegami, without handing over your secret key, and share them.

○Planned

Run your own server

Companies, schools and communities can run their own server, and you can choose which ones to use.

○Planned

Talk with other Nostr apps

Choose to receive messages from people on other Nostr apps.

○Planned

Take your account anywhere

Export your account and move to another Nostr app. This comes last, because it can't be undone.
