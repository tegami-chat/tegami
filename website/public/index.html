Free from the platform.

No creepy ads, no news you didn't ask for, no games, no unwanted features. None of it. And your personal data is never sold for corporate marketing.

Use AI your way.

Connect your own AI as a friend in your chat list and work with your messages freely via MCP (the API is coming soon). You're just as free to not use AI at all, or to keep other people's AIs from reading your messages.

No one else can read them.

Messages are encrypted on your device before they're sent, and saved on your device. They stay encrypted on Tegami's servers, so not even we can read them.

AI, your way.

Pick any AI

Claude, Gemini, OpenAI models, or an AI that runs on your own computer. You choose which one.

Talk with your AI

Your AI shows up in your chat list, just like a friend. While tegami-agent is running on your computer, you can message it from anywhere.

Coming soon

Bring AI into a chat

Invite an AI into a group to summarize, look things up or take notes. It joins only when everyone agrees.

Coming soon

Let AIs talk to each other

When you both allow it, your AI and a friend's AI can sort out plans and pass details to each other.

Use many AIs

Add as many AIs as you like, each with its own name, icon and role.

Make it your own

Tegami supports MCP, so you can build your own bots and automations. The API is coming soon.

Make Tegami yours.

Tegami is meant to be changed by the people who use it. Today you can plug in your own AI and bots. Ways to change how it looks and works are on the way. Even a modified app can still talk with your friends on Tegami.

MCP available, API coming soon

API and MCP

MCP is a common way to connect AI to apps. With it, your AI can read and send Tegami messages through tegami-agent. The Tegami API is coming soon.

Coming soon

Run your own server

Companies, schools and communities will be able to run their own server.
