Weekly usage

An envelope is one encrypted message on its way. Each message makes one envelope for every recipient and one copy for the sender, so a message to one person is two envelopes.

Only weeks that are over are shown. A week starts on Monday at 00:00 UTC. To keep quiet weeks from pointing to anyone, every figure is rounded up to a multiple of 10, and 0 to 9 is shown as "under 10".

    How we count

    Each day, the relay counts the envelopes it accepted, the number of files, the number of logins, and an estimate of how many accounts logged in.

    The number of accounts is estimated with a random value that is made and thrown away every day, so the statistics can't show which accounts were active (the method is called HyperLogLog). For the same reason, the account figure is the average per day, not a weekly total. Envelopes, files and logins are weekly totals. We don't collect message contents, or which screens you open in the app.

    For what the relay stores and for how long, see "What the relay keeps" in our privacy policy.
