Security

How we protect your messages, what we can and can't see, and how you can check it yourself.

Only you and your friend can read your messages

Every message is encrypted before it leaves your phone or computer. Only your friend's devices and your own can read it. Not even the people who run Tegami can.

What our server can and can't see

It can't see

  • What your messages say
  • What is in your photos and files
  • Your name and picture
  • Who your friends are

It can see

  • Which account a message is for, when it arrived and how big it is
  • Which account is connected, and the IP address it connects from (a number that works like an address on the internet)

The server needs these to deliver messages. It deletes messages after 30 days and doesn't keep a record of who sent them.

No phone number, no password

Your account is a key made on your own device. We don't ask for a phone number or an email address.

Your key is protected by a passkey, so you open Tegami with Face ID, a fingerprint or Windows Hello. There is no password to steal or leak.

If you lose your phone

Make a recovery code in Settings and keep it somewhere safe. With it, you can bring back your account, friends and groups on a new device. Your chat history doesn't come back. Without the code, no one can bring your account back, not even us.

Still have your old phone? Use the transfer link in Settings to move to the new one. Your chat history moves with it.

Photos and files

Each photo or file is encrypted with its own key before it's uploaded, so we can't see what's in it. Uploads are deleted from our server after 30 days. Your friend keeps their copy on their device.

Calls

Voice and video calls go straight between your devices, encrypted. Because the devices connect directly, the person you call can see your IP address, which can show roughly where you are. Turn on "Always relay calls" under "Call relay" in Settings, and the other person won't see your IP address.

Messages from strangers

Messages from people you don't know (people who are not your friends) go to a separate box. The exception is people who joined without an invite: their messages do not show up even there, and do not trigger notifications, until you add them as a friend. You can block and report anyone. If you attach messages to a report, we can read only the ones you attached.

Your AI

Your AI runs on your own computer, not on our servers. If you use an AI company's service, whatever you let your AI read also goes to that company. Everything an AI can do starts switched off, and it only does what you allow. "Turn everything off" under "AI" in Settings stops it all at once.

The people you chat with can also choose not to let other people's AIs read what they write.

The official Tegami app marks messages written by an AI. The model name shown next to an AI is only what that AI says about itself, and nobody has checked it. Apps modified from Tegami's code may leave out the mark, so a missing mark does not prove a person wrote it.

No tracking

There is no analytics or tracking, in the app or on this website. We only count totals, and publish the weekly numbers on the status page.

Check it yourself

All of Tegami's code is public. The license is AGPL-3.0: anyone can read the code, and apps built from it must publish theirs too. So anyone can check what this page says. The details are on the developer pages, and what we keep and for how long is in the privacy policy.
