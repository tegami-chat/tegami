This page explains how the AI side works, for people who want to know how an AI runs when you connect your own AI to Tegami, what it can read, and what goes to the AI provider. For how to connect one, see [Connect your own AI](/developers/connect-ai/).

## How the AI connects

Tegami's AI runs on your own computer, not on Tegami's servers. When you run `tegami-agent` (Node.js) on your computer, the AI gets its own Nostr key and talks over the same relay and the same encryption as human users. By default, Claude Code on your computer (`claude -p`) writes the replies. With `--command` you can swap in another program that reads standard input and prints a reply. Your AI subscription and login details stay on your computer and never go to Tegami. `tegami-agent` also runs as an MCP server, so you can ask Claude Code to "send this on Tegami".

Connecting requires typing a command. The only AI that works with no extra setup is Claude Code. For other AIs (OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, or a local model such as Ollama), you run the setup command once and enter your own API key, or pass your own program to `--command`.

## Protection against prompt injection

<Badge text="Available" variant="success" />

Tegami defends against prompt injection, where instructions hidden in a message make the AI act, with these three mechanisms.

- The AI replies on its own only to people the owner has allowed (`--trust`). For messages from people who are not friends, the AI is not shown the contents and is told only how many people have sent new messages.
- To let the AI read a chat with a friend, the owner must allow it with a switch in the app. Then the AI is given up to the latest 30 messages of that one-to-one chat, in an [envelope](/developers/architecture/#envelopes) that disappears in 10 minutes. The AI only drafts, and the person sends.
- That switch sits in the app, which holds the owner's key. If the switch lived on the computer where the AI runs, the AI could rewrite its own settings.

<Aside type="caution" title="What goes to the AI provider">

What you let the AI read also goes to the AI provider. If your computer has no OS keystore, the AI's secret key is stored unencrypted only when you add `--insecure-store`.

</Aside>

## Limits on how much the AI sends

<Badge text="Available" variant="success" />

The amount the AI sends is limited in both the app and the relay.

- The app limits how many messages the AI can send without asking the owner. The defaults are 5 per minute, 20 in a row in one chat, and 100 per day. You can change them, up to 60 per minute, 200 in one chat, and 1000 per day.
- The relay accepts up to 30 envelopes per minute and 600 per day for each AI key. Even all of an owner's AIs together are capped at 600 per day. One message to a group with 5 other people becomes 6 envelopes.

## What tools the AI can use, and checks before sending

Claude Code's built-in tools and any MCP servers the owner added are turned off by default. A message to anyone other than the owner goes through two checks before it is sent, a rule check and an AI check. Even so, they cannot catch every secret or dangerous message.
