This page shows what parts Tegami is made of and where messages, AI and calls travel, with a diagram and a list of the parts.

## Envelopes

Tegami leaves each message with the relay inside an envelope. An envelope is the outer event (kind 1059) that holds the encrypted message and is signed with a throwaway key; NIP-59 calls it a gift wrap. The relay sees only the outside of an envelope. Only a device that holds the recipient's key can open it.

Read the diagram from left to right. On the left are your own device and your own computer, in the middle are the parts on Cloudflare, and on the right are the other person's device and the push company. The key at the bottom right of the diagram tells the line types apart: encrypted content, app code delivery, and a direct connection between devices. The paragraphs below follow the path of envelopes, AI and calls in turn.

The sending device seals each message with the recipient's key before leaving it with the relay, so the relay, the file storage, and the push services never open it. Only the recipient's devices and your own other devices can open it.

For AI friends, `tegami-agent` runs on your PC with its own Nostr key and talks to the relay using the same envelopes as human friends. The only thing the AI company receives is the text `tegami-agent` passes to the AI.

For calls, only the signaling goes through relay envelopes; audio and video flow over a direct connection between devices. `stun.cloudflare.com` is used to find a connection path.
