This page explains one by one how notifications, calls, and photos and files work, for people who want to know the mechanism behind each.

## Notifications

<Badge text="Available" variant="success" />

Tegami notifications use Web Push. A device signs its notification address and notification key with its own key and leaves them with the relay. When an [envelope](/developers/architecture/#envelopes) arrives, the relay sends a payload encrypted for each device (RFC 8291). The payload holds only the kind ("message or incoming call"), the destination account, and a count, and never the sender or the text. However, to tell calls apart, only the envelope that rings the other person carries a `t=call` tag, so the relay can tell that an envelope was a call.

You cannot turn off notifications for one chat. Neither the relay nor the device's notification mechanism knows which chat an arriving envelope is for. On the iPhone browser version, an incoming call does not take over the full screen like a normal phone call.

- <Badge text="Decided" variant="note" /> When we build the iPhone app, we will add the full-screen incoming call display (Apple CallKit).

## Calls

<Badge text="Available" variant="success" />

Tegami calls are one-to-one voice and video calls over WebRTC. Signaling such as ringing and answering passes through the relay in encrypted envelopes that expire in 1 minute. STUN (`stun.cloudflare.com`) is used to find a connection path. When devices cannot connect directly, or when you choose "Always relay calls", the call goes through a relay server (TURN). Audio and video stay encrypted even when they pass through a relay server.

You can use Cloudflare's relay server, or set up one you run yourself. If you choose "Always relay calls", the other person cannot see your IP address. In a direct call, the other person can see your IP address. There are no group calls.

## Photos and files

<Badge text="Available" variant="success" />

Tegami encrypts each photo and file with a new AES-GCM key and then leaves it in the relay's storage (Cloudflare R2). The key goes inside the encrypted message and reaches only the other person. Data in storage is deleted after 30 days, but stays on the device of whoever received it. Photos are shrunk to 1600px on the long side before sending, and the file size limit is 10MB after encryption.

Stickers are handled differently. So that the ciphertext is the same on every device of yours, the key is made from the image and your account's secret key, and the sticker stays in separate storage until you delete it.

You cannot send in original quality yet.

- <Badge text="Decided" variant="note" /> The sender will be able to choose between sending small and sending in original quality.
- <Badge text="Under discussion" variant="caution" /> We have not decided whether sending in original quality will be a paid feature.
