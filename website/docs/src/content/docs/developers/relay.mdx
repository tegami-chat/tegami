This page explains how Tegami's relay works, for people who want to know what it runs on, who can register, and what it counts. To run your own relay, see [Run your own relay](/developers/self-host-relay/).

## What the relay runs on

<Badge text="Available" variant="success" />

Tegami's relay runs on Cloudflare Workers and a [Durable Object](/developers/spec/#glossary) (SQLite). The code is in `relay/`. It accepts only [envelopes](/developers/architecture/#envelopes) (kind 1059) addressed to a single person, and you can read only envelopes addressed to you. Envelopes are deleted after 30 days, and sending is limited per IP address and per key.

The only official relay is the one Tegami runs.

- <Badge text="Decided" variant="note" /> We start by keeping envelopes for 30 days. If few people turn out to stay away for long, we will shorten it based on how the app is used.

## Registration and who can use it

<Badge text="Available" variant="success" />

Anyone can register with Tegami's relay. You register with a `POST /register` signed with [NIP-98](/developers/spec/#glossary), and pass either an invite ticket or a check that you are human (Cloudflare Turnstile). A key that is not registered cannot write.

Messages from someone who registered without an invite ticket are not shown anywhere, including the "From people you don't know" section, and no notification sounds, until you add them as a friend. They appear from the messages that arrive after the other person is added through a QR code or an invite link. Even a modified app can use the relay if its key passed registration, so what protects the relay is the registration gate, not the app.

There is no verification badge (a mark of authenticity) yet.

- <Badge text="Decided" variant="note" /> Impersonation will be stopped with several methods combined. Family and friends read each other's QR codes in person, companies and shops are checked by domain, and people who want a mark of authenticity can get one for a fee through an outside identity-verification service. Even then Tegami receives only the result ("verified") and holds no personal information such as an address.

## Usage counts

<Badge text="Available" variant="success" />

The relay keeps daily totals for 183 days. What it counts is envelopes received, the number and size of files stored, the number of logins, and the approximate number of keys that logged in that day. Only the operator can see the daily numbers.

What we publish is only the totals for finished weeks, with the latest 24 weeks on [Uptime and usage statistics](/status). Published numbers are shown as 0 for 0 to 9, and rounded up to a multiple of 10 for 10 or more. The published number of keys is the daily average.

The number of keys that logged in is estimated with [HyperLogLog](/developers/spec/#glossary). Before counting, a value that changes every day (a [salt](/developers/spec/#glossary)) is mixed in, and that value is discarded when the day changes. Neither the app nor the website has any analytics.

- <Badge text="Decided" variant="note" /> We will not add analytics that follow what people do.
