Where Tegami is different

You can start without giving a phone number. There are no ads and no company AI. Your own AI can sit in your chat list like a friend.

Where Tegami falls short

On encryption strength alone, Signal is ahead. Signal keeps changing its keys, so a stolen key doesn't expose past messages. Tegami doesn't have that yet.

Tegami runs only in the browser for now. Apps for iPhone, Android and computers are on the way.

Far fewer people use Tegami than LINE or WhatsApp, so your friends need to install it too.

Switching

To move to Tegami, add friends by sending an invite link or showing a QR code. There is no way to bring in your LINE chat history.

You can export your Tegami chat history to a file from Settings at any time. Moving your Tegami account to another app isn't possible yet.

Where your chats are kept

Tegami keeps your chats on your device. The server holds messages for up to 30 days, then deletes them.

Other apps' policies can change. If you spot a mistake, please tell us in a GitHub issue. A more detailed comparison is on the developer page.
