Privacy policy

This privacy policy ("this policy") explains how Yoshihiro Azuma ("the operator", "we") handles user information in the messaging app Tegami ("the service").

1. Scope

  • tegami.chat (website)
  • app.tegami.chat (web app)
  • relay.tegami.chat (relay)
  • The iPhone and Android apps, once released

Relays and apps that other people run with Tegami's open-source code are not covered. Neither is an AI friend you run on your own computer.

2. What the operator can't see

Messages are encrypted on your device before they are sent. Only the sender's and recipients' devices hold the keys needed to decrypt them. As a result, the operator cannot see the following:

  • The text of messages
  • The contents of photos, videos, files, voice messages and stamps
  • Who sent a message
  • Your list of friends
  • Your name and icon
  • Call audio and video
  • Your secret key (which remains encrypted even when sent outside your device)

Exceptions: the operator can see the text and the sender of messages attached to a report. The relay also keeps who invited whom for 90 days, and which account keeps which stamps (the stamp images stay encrypted).

3. What the relay keeps

The relay is the server that delivers messages. The information it collects and stores is as follows.

Registered accounts

What

Public key, whether the account was registered with an invite, registration date and time, and whether the account is suspended

Purpose

To allow only registered accounts to send messages, and to suspend abusive accounts

How long

Until the account is deleted

Message envelopes

What

The encrypted message (an "envelope": each message makes one for every recipient and one copy for the sender), the recipient's public key, the arrival time, and the data size

Purpose

To deliver messages later when a device is off or offline

How long

30 days. Envelopes for moving an account to a new device: 10 minutes

Photos, videos, files and voice messages

What

Encrypted data. The uploader is not recorded

Purpose

To deliver them to recipients

How long

30 days

Stamps

What

The encrypted stamp image, the public key of the account that keeps it, its size, and when it was stored

Purpose

So you can send the same stamp again and use it on all your devices, and so only the account that keeps a stamp can delete it

How long

Until you delete the stamp. After deletion, copies may remain in caches for up to one day

Invite records

What

Who invited whom

Purpose

To suspend accounts that engage in abuse, along with the accounts they invited

How long

90 days

Reports

What

The public keys of the reporting and reported accounts, and the reason for the report. If the reporter chooses, up to 20 messages from the reported user, decrypted on the reporter's device. The reported user is not notified

Purpose

To stop abuse

How long

90 days

Notification addresses

What

Your device’s notification address, the keys to encrypt notifications to it, and the public key of the account using it

Purpose

To tell you about new messages and calls

How long

Until you turn notifications off

Recovery backups

What

A backup of your account, encrypted with your recovery code. The relay cannot tell which account it belongs to

Purpose

To restore your account if you lose your device

How long

Until you delete it

Usage statistics

What

Daily counts of envelopes, files and their total size, logins, and the estimated number of accounts that logged in. Public keys are not stored

Purpose

To understand how Tegami is used and how many servers we need

How long

6 months. Only the operator can see the daily counts. What we publish is the total for each week that is over, rounded up to a multiple of 10 (0 to 9 shown as 0). The last 24 weeks are public at relay.tegami.chat/stats

Error reports

What

The error message, where in the code it occurred, the app version, and the device and browser type. Quoted text and long strings are removed on your device before sending. Keys and chat messages are not included. Sent only if you turn this on in settings (off by default)

Purpose

To fix bugs

How long

Not stored by the relay. Up to 7 days in Cloudflare’s logs

IP addresses

What

The address you connect from

Purpose

To limit the number of connections and the amount of data sent from a single network, and to verify that you are not a bot when you register without an invite

How long

Held in memory only while you’re connected. Never stored

4. What stays on your device

The following are saved only on your device.

  • Messages, photos, videos, files, voice messages and stamps
  • Friends, groups, names and icons
  • Your secret key, encrypted with your passkey or a key inside your browser

Clearing your browser's site data deletes Tegami's data on that device. You can export your message history from settings.

5. Companies that handle data

Tegami uses the services below. None of them can read your encrypted messages.

Cloudflare, Inc.

Country

United States

Purpose

Runs the website, the app, the relay and file storage

What it receives

Your IP address when you connect, and the data handled by the relay. Messages (except those attached to reports), files and recovery backups remain encrypted

Cloudflare, Inc.

Country

United States

Purpose

Verifies that you are not a bot when you register without an invite (Turnstile)

What it receives

Your IP address and information about your browser

Cloudflare, Inc.

Country

United States

Purpose

Establishes a connection path between devices during calls (STUN)

What it receives

Your IP address

Apple, Google, Mozilla, Microsoft

Country

United States

Purpose

Deliver notifications to your device

What it receives

Your notification address and when a notification was sent. The notification content is encrypted so only your device can decrypt it. It contains only whether it is a message or a call, which account it is for, and the number of new messages

Cloudflare's servers are located in the United States and other countries. The United States has no comprehensive federal privacy law like Japan's, and some US laws allow the government to order companies to hand over data. Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission publishes an overview of the US legal system.

6. Calls

Call audio and video are encrypted and sent directly between devices without passing through the operator's servers. Because of this, your IP address is visible to the other person on the call.

7. AI friends

An AI friend is a program you run on your own computer. Messages with it also pass through the relay in encrypted envelopes.

Messages you let the AI read are sent to the AI service you use and handled under that service's terms. Tegami's encryption no longer protects them.

8. What we never do

  • Sell your personal information
  • Show ads
  • Track you (we do not use analytics in the app or on the website)
  • Use cookies on tegami.chat
  • Share personal information with third parties without your consent, except when required by law

9. Requests from police and other authorities

We only respond to requests that we are legally required to comply with under Japanese law. We can only provide the information listed in section 3. Except for messages attached to reports, we cannot provide the text of messages because we cannot read it.

We will publish the number of requests each year. As of November 15, 2026, there have been none.

10. Requests about your personal data

What you can do yourself:

  • Turn notifications off: the relay deletes your notification address and keys
  • Tap "Delete recovery code" in settings: the relay deletes your recovery backup
  • Envelopes and files other than stamps are deleted automatically after 30 days
  • Delete a stamp: the relay deletes the stamp image and the record of who kept it

To ask for the purpose of use of information linked to your public key (such as registration, invite and report records, notification addresses, or stamps), or to request its disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, or cessation of sharing with third parties, email hello@tegami.chat with your account's public key. We disclose information by email.

  • To verify your identity, the operator will send a Tegami message to that public key
  • If you have lost your device, or if you are making a request as a legal representative (such as a parent or guardian) or an authorized agent, please say so in your email. We will let you know how to verify your identity
  • Records needed to prevent abuse, such as a record that an account was suspended, may not be deleted
  • Files other than stamps, and recovery backups, are not linked to your public key, so we cannot identify them from your public key. Stamps are linked to your public key, so requests for disclosure and deletion include them
  • We use your email only to handle your request, and delete it within a year after we finish
  • There is no fee for making a request

We plan to add an option to delete your account directly within the app.

11. Children

Tegami doesn't check anyone's age. Children under 13 should use Tegami with a parent or guardian.

In the European Union, children under 16 need a parent or guardian's consent to turn on notifications or error reports. If their country sets a lower age, that age applies.

12. How we protect your data

  • Messages, photos, videos, files, voice messages, stamps and recovery backups are encrypted on your device before they are sent, except messages attached to reports
  • All connections to the website, the app and the relay are encrypted
  • The relay only accepts messages in encrypted envelopes
  • The relay gives each logged-in account only the envelopes addressed to it
  • Administrative actions, such as suspending an account, can only be carried out when signed with the operator's key
  • Data stored on the relay is automatically deleted once its storage period expires
  • All code is public on GitHub. You can check what the relay stores in relay/src. However, there is not yet a way to verify from the outside that the running relay uses that exact code
  • Only the operator handles the operator's administrative key and report records

13. If you live in the European Union

The operator complies with the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The legal bases for processing your data are:

  • Necessary to provide the service: registering your public key, delivering messages and files, connecting calls, storing stamps, and storing recovery backups
  • Legitimate interests: preventing abuse, limiting the number of connections and the amount of data sent, and compiling usage statistics
  • Your consent: notifications and error reports. You can withdraw consent in settings at any time. Withdrawing does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal

Using the service requires a public key and your IP address while connected, and registering also requires an invite or bot verification. We do not make automated decisions about you or build profiles. The European Commission recognizes Japan as providing an adequate level of data protection.

You have the right to access, correct, delete and export your data, to restrict or object to how it is processed, and to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

14. If you live in California

Tegami doesn't track you across websites and has no ads or analytics from other companies. A Do Not Track setting in your browser therefore doesn't change anything Tegami does.

15. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we add the date of the change at the end. The full change history is on GitHub. Important changes are announced in advance on tegami.chat and Tegami's official accounts.

If the Japanese and English versions differ, the Japanese version applies.
