Only your friend can open your messages

Before a message leaves your phone or computer, Tegami locks it and seals it in an envelope. Only your friend's devices, and your own, can open it. Not even the people who run Tegami can read it.

What our server can and can't see

It can't see

  • What your messages say
  • What is in your photos and files
  • Your name and picture
  • Who your friends are

It can see

  • Which account an envelope is for, when it arrived and how big it is
  • Which account is connected, and the network address (IP address) it connects from

The server needs these to deliver messages. It deletes envelopes after 30 days and doesn't keep who sent them.

No phone number, no password

Your account is a key made on your own device. We don't ask for a phone number or an email address.

The key is protected by a passkey, your device's built-in way of checking it's you, so you unlock Tegami with Face ID, a fingerprint or Windows Hello. There is no password to steal or leak.

If you lose your phone

Make a recovery code in Settings and keep it somewhere safe. With it you can bring your account, friends and groups back on a new device. Without it, no one can bring your account back, not even us. Your chat history doesn't come back with the recovery code.

Still have your old phone? Use the transfer link in Settings to move to the new one. Your chat history moves with it.

Photos and files

Each photo or file is locked with its own key before it's uploaded, so our storage only holds scrambled data. Uploads are deleted after 30 days. Your friend keeps their copy on their device.

Calls

Voice and video go straight between your devices, encrypted. Because the two devices connect directly, the person you call can see your network address (IP address), which can show roughly where you are.

Strangers and unwanted messages

Messages from people you don't know land in a separate box. You can block and report anyone. If you attach messages to a report, we can read the ones you attached.

Your AI

Your AI runs on your own computer, not on our servers. Everything you let it read also goes to the company behind that AI. Everything AI can do starts switched off, and only what you allow will work.

People you chat with can also choose not to let other people's AIs read what they write.

The official Tegami app marks messages written by an AI. The model name shown next to an AI is what that AI says about itself. Apps modified from Tegami's code may leave out the mark.

No tracking

There is no analytics or tracking, in the app or on this website. We only count totals, and publish the weekly numbers on the status page.

What isn't done yet

  • If someone steals your key, they could also read past messages still on the server or your device. That's why the key is locked with a passkey.
  • No outside security audit yet.
  • The browser version loads its code each time you open it, so whoever serves it could swap in different code without you noticing.
  • You can't talk with people on other Nostr apps yet.

Check it yourself

All of Tegami's code is public under AGPL-3.0, so you don't have to take our word for any of this. The details are on the developer pages, and what we keep and for how long is in the privacy policy.
