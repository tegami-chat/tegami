Frequently asked questions

Is it free? Who pays for it?+

Yes, it's free, with no ads. Tegami is made by an independent developer in Japan, with no outside investment. The developer currently covers the costs, and support from users and supporters is welcome.

Who can read my messages?+

Only you and the people you send them to. Messages are encrypted on your device, so not even Tegami can read them. There are two exceptions. If you allow an AI to read a chat, that AI can read it, and what it reads also goes to the company behind it. You can choose not to let other people’s AIs read your messages. If you attach messages to a report, the operator can read the ones you attached.

Do I need a phone number or email?+

No. Just pick a name and start.

Which devices can I use?+

Any web browser, on your phone or computer, today. Apps for iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and Linux are on the way.

How do I add friends?+

Send your invite link, or show your QR code. Your friends need Tegami too.

What if I lose my phone?+

Make a recovery code in Settings, and you can bring your account, friends and groups back on a new device. Your chat history does not come back. Without it, no one, not even Tegami, can restore it.

Can I use it on my phone and computer at the same time?+

Yes. Use the transfer link in Settings to use the same account on another device at the same time. Your earlier chats move over too, newest first. If there are very many, the oldest stay on the old device. Photos and files open on the new device only while they are still stored on the server.

What does Tegami collect?+

The statistics count only totals, like how many messages were delivered in a week, and never anyone's activity. The weekly numbers are public on the status page. We also keep records of sign-ups, invites and reports for a set time. The kinds and periods are in the privacy policy.

How do I connect my AI?+

Today, run the small tegami-agent program on your computer and add it with its invite link. Your AI then shows up in your chat list. Scan the QR code shown on your computer with the app, and your AI is added as a friend.

What about unwanted messages?+

Messages from strangers land in a separate box, and you can block and report anyone.

What if Tegami shuts down?+

The code is public, so someone with the skills can run their own server and keep Tegami going. But the app cannot yet connect to a different server, and you cannot yet move your account to another Nostr app. You can export your chats anytime.

Where are the technical details?+

On the developer page: which parts of Nostr Tegami uses, what the relay can see, how keys are kept, and the full spec. The code is on GitHub.
