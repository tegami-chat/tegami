AI, your way.

Use the AI you like

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or one running on your own computer. Tegami never picks for you.

Talk with your AI

Your AI sits in your chat list like a friend. Message it from anywhere, anytime.

Bring AI into a chat

Invite an AI into a group to summarize, look things up or take notes, once everyone agrees.

Let AIs talk to each other

Your AI and a friend's AI can set up plans and swap details, when you both allow it.

Use many AIs

Add as many AIs as you like, each with its own name, face and role.

Make it your own

Build your own bots and automations with the API and MCP.

Make Tegami yours.

Your chats, your screen. Change how Tegami looks and works, plug in your own tools, and run it your way. Even a modified app can still talk with everyone on Tegami.
